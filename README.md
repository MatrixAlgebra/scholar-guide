## Notes

- Cancels
	- Hammer Spin (shift+c)
		- Make sure you have spacebar or c to cancel out of it,, you'll likely get grabbed
		- You can bait grabs in shift+c and instantly spacebar to iframe it and possibly kd, after iframe changes it's a pretty tight window, so not super safe
	- Gold Continnuum (Magnus)
		- Always cancel out of the end of the magnus with c, it's too slow otherwise
	- Hammer Smash (shift+lmb) 
		- can backdash or c in first half for a quick catch attempt
		- if you dont cancel during first atk you get stuck
	- Infinite Power (shift+rmb)
		- Always want to cancel this, as the charge up doesn't really do a lot of damage, and the cancelled version does full damage
		- s+rmb > flow > shift+rmb
		- rmb > flow > shift+rmb
		- (any move that uses small hammers, w+f, dash, etc) > c > shift+rmb
		- f > flow > shift+rmb
		- also cancels out of golden blockade (w+e flow) but lock that shit
- Movement
	- If you randomly get a cc but are too far away you can Gravity Rush (shift+x) to close distance and possibly stiff
	- You can camera turn the second half of Hammer Rush (w+c)
	- Q -> Q can get behind forward guard (its slower now though)
- Lingers and SA
	- You can linger shift+s for a longer iframe
	- s+f (hold) is unprot at the start buff but lingers SA a long time
	- Can hold shift+rmb to stall in SA, weave and rotate through s+e, shift+c, shift+s, shift+z and s+lmb
- Misc Tips
	- Careful with holding s+lmb, you're really easy to grab out of it and esp if you hold it for magnus
	- Put repulsion on hotbar so you can use it to take out weaps
	-  Home Run (s+lmb) flows out of Hammer Throw (s+f) to change damage area from a line to a cone, useful for PvP stiff -> Grab combo extension.
